Keep your team connected throughout the season. The Squad brings schedules, rosters, scores and team conversations together for coaches, athletes and families.

KNOW WHAT IS NEXT
View upcoming games, practices and team events. Find the team information you need before heading out.

KEEP YOUR TEAM TOGETHER
Access your roster and player information, follow scores and updates, and stay connected through team conversations.

USE YOUR TEAM ACCOUNT
Sign in with your existing account or create a free account. Join the correct team using your coach or administrator's invitation. Features depend on your role, team membership and plan.

The Squad supports sports programs with multiple teams and roles. This iPhone and iPad app gives you access to the mobile features available to your account; web and mobile features may differ.

An internet connection is required. Free accounts and paid plans are available. Any subscription offered in the app shows its price and billing period before purchase. Manage an existing subscription through the provider used to purchase it.
